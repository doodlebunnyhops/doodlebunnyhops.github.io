The Blue Key Card can be found in two locations:

- The hidden attic room at the Police Station
- The Power Room inside the Power Plant

Either card can be used to access [Brookhaven Electric](/lore/quests/electric_building/) and the hidden [Power Plant Agency Room](/lore/quests/meltdown/#power-plant-agency-room).

## Location 1: Police Station

### Steps

1. Go to the Police Station.
2. Enter the second jail cell.
3. Climb onto the top bunk and jump through the vent.
4. Go through the hidden wall on the right side.
5. Grab the Blue Key Card behind the cabinet.

## Location 2: Power Plant

### Steps

1. Go inside the Power Plant.
2. Head upstairs to the walkway above the reactors.
3. Look for the three upstairs rooms. From left to right, they are:
   - Living Quarters
   - Control Room
   - Power Room
4. Enter the **Power Room** on the right.
5. Look underneath the chair to find the Blue Key Card.
